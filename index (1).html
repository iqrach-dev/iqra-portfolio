Get to know me

📍 Haripur, Pakistan · University of Haripur

I'm a BS Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Haripur, building my foundation in Python and C++ through coursework, internships, and a fair amount of trial and error.

I like problems that start out messy — a pile of requirements, an unclear spec — and turning them into something that runs cleanly.

AI & ML Python Developer Technical Writer Problem Solver

During my internship at Skylark Company, I worked on three projects — a rule-based offline chatbot, an online quiz system, and a SaaS-based AI parking management system. Each came with its own set of problems, and solving them taught me how to think in code rather than just write it.

Outside of development, I work as a freelance content writer and proofreader, helping clients turn rough drafts into clear, readable copy.

I spent a year teaching Math, Urdu, Computer Science, and Islamiyat — which turned out to be some of the best training I've had in explaining complicated things simply.

That habit carried into how I write about code: fewer words, more clarity. If a sentence (or a function) can't explain itself, it gets rewritten.
