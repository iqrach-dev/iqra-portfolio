Every project starts as a mess of requirements. My job is to take that apart, piece by piece, until it's something that actually runs.

I'm a BS Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Haripur, building my foundation in Python and C++ through coursework, internships, and a fair amount of trial and error.

During my internship at Skylark Company, I worked on three projects — a rule-based offline chatbot, an online quiz system, and a SaaS-based AI parking management system. Each came with its own set of problems, and solving them taught me how to think in code rather than just write it.

Outside of development, I work as a freelance content writer and proofreader, and spent a year teaching Math, Urdu, Computer Science, and Islamiyat — which turned out to be some of the best training I've had in explaining complicated things simply.
